Returns & Refunds

Last updated: August 2026

Returns

We want you to be satisfied with your MAVRENX purchase.

If you would like to return an eligible item, contact MAVRENX Support as soon as possible after receiving your order.

Where applicable under consumer law, customers may have the right to withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days after receiving the goods.

Return Conditions

Returned products should normally be:

  • In their original condition
  • Unused beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect the product
  • Returned with original accessories and components where applicable
  • Packaged securely for return shipping

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, incomplete or different from what you ordered, contact us as soon as possible.

Please include your MAVRENX order number and clear photos or other information showing the problem.

We will review the issue and determine the appropriate solution, which may include a replacement, refund or another remedy where required.

Refunds

Once a return or refund request has been approved, the refund will normally be sent back to the original payment method.

Payment providers and banks may take additional time to display the refunded amount after the refund has been processed.

Order Cancellations

If you need to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible after placing it.

If the order has not yet entered fulfillment, we may be able to cancel it before shipment.

If the order has already been processed or shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible and the normal return process may apply.

Return Shipping

Return shipping arrangements depend on the reason for the return and the circumstances of the order.

Contact MAVRENX before returning a product so we can provide the correct instructions and return destination.

Do not send a product to an address without receiving return instructions from us first.

Non-Returnable Items

Certain products may not be eligible for return where an exception is permitted by applicable consumer law.

This can include certain personalised, hygiene-sensitive, sealed or perishable items depending on the product and circumstances.

Any applicable exception will be handled in accordance with consumer protection law.

Your Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or limit any mandatory consumer rights that apply to you under applicable law.
