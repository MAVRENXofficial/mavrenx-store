Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

MAVRENX respects your privacy and aims to handle personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the MAVRENX website, place an order, contact support or use order tracking and notification features.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use MAVRENX, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Order details and order status
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support
  • Technical information needed for website functionality, security and notifications

Payments

MAVRENX uses third-party payment providers, including Stripe, to process payments.

Payment card information is entered through the payment provider's secure checkout system. MAVRENX does not need to store your full card number on this website.

Payment providers may process personal information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

How We Use Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Process and manage orders
  • Arrange delivery and fulfillment
  • Provide order tracking
  • Send order-status notifications
  • Respond to customer questions
  • Prevent fraud and protect the website
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Improve MAVRENX services and website functionality

Suppliers and Service Providers

Information may be shared with trusted third parties when necessary to provide your order or operate the website.

This can include payment processors, fulfillment partners, suppliers, shipping carriers, website infrastructure providers and other service providers.

We aim to share only the information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant service.

Order Notifications

If you choose to enable browser notifications, MAVRENX may use a notification token associated with your browser or device to send updates about your order.

You can disable website notifications through your browser or device settings.

Cookies and Local Storage

MAVRENX may use browser storage, such as local storage, to support features including your shopping cart, liked products, theme preferences and notification settings.

Third-party services used by the website may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to complete orders, provide customer support, maintain required business records, prevent fraud and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information.

However, no online system or method of data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information. These may include rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other actions where provided by applicable law.

If you want to make a privacy-related request, contact MAVRENX using the email address below.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when MAVRENX services, technology or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
